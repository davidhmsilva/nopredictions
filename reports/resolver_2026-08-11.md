## Why nothing resolved

I probed all 27 due markets directly against the Gamma and CLOB APIs. **Every one**
returns `closed: false`, `active: true`, `archived: false`, `umaResolutionStatus: null`,
and no `winner` flag on either token. The resolver requires a genuine settlement
signal before writing a result, so it correctly declined all 27.

I did not settle them by hand. Three findings say hand-settling would have written
wrong results:

**1. Market title dates disagree with actual kickoff.**
- `"Will Águilas Doradas Rionegro win on 2026-08-09?"` → `gameStartTime` is **2026-08-10 21:00**
- `"Will Tigres FC win on 2026-08-10?"` → `gameStartTime` is 2026-08-10 19:00, but a
  third-party schedule (xscores) puts that fixture at **2026-08-11 19:00 UTC** — tonight.

Settling on the title date settles the wrong fixture. This is the same clock-drift class
that produced the unusable `obs_version = 1` late-goals data.

**2. `pm_markets.resolution_time` is a stale snapshot.**
Trade 1698 (Chicago Fire vs Vancouver Whitecaps, "Neither team to score first") shows
`resolution_time = 2026-07-17` in our DB. Gamma now has `gameStartTime = 2026-10-07` —
**the fixture was postponed.** That market is legitimately still open and must not be
resolved. It is only in the "stuck" bucket because our copy of the date never refreshed.

**3. api-football was unavailable as a referee.**
`FOOTBALL_API_KEY` returned *"You have reached the request limit for the day"* — the
24/7 late-goals observer burns the daily quota. So there was no quota-free way to confirm
a score or a corner count independently.

## Recommended follow-ups (not actioned — need your call)

1. **Void, don't guess.** For the 14 stuck corner markets plus the frozen Uruguayan ones,
   add a `result = 'void'` path (stake returned, excluded from yield and CLV) rather than
   forcing won/lost. They are ~21u of dead stake distorting the open book.
2. **Refresh `resolution_time` on every poll.** Trade 1698 proves a fixture can move by
   three months while our snapshot keeps the original date, which silently mislabels a
   live trade as overdue.
3. **Trust `gameStartTime`, never the title date.** The title is free text and is already
   wrong on at least two live markets.
4. **Give the resolver its own api-football quota** (or a separate key), so settlement is
   not starved by the late-goals observer.
5. **Add a staleness alarm** — any trade unresolved >72h past `gameStartTime` should
   surface, instead of accumulating quietly for 25 days.
