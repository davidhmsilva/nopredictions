## Is the edge real?

Bootstrapped over 91 events (resampled by event, not by lot, because lots inside one fixture are the same bet taken repeatedly): yield +10.68%, 95% CI [-13.76%, +35.81%], p(≤0) = 0.2003. **The interval contains zero.** Whatever this wallet's headline profit, its record does not distinguish it from a wallet that got lucky at this size.

Concentration: top event 40% of profit, top 5 146%, top 10 237%; 56% of events profitable. Drop the 200 best individual lots and the rest of the book LOSES $142,915 (-43.17%) — the entire result is those 200 trades. **A large share of the result is a handful of bets** — treat the headline as one draw.

Reconciliation: Polymarket says $41,098 all-time, counted before fees; on the same basis (P&L plus $5,876 of fees paid) we reconstruct $41,243 — a gap of -$144.92 (0.4%). Close enough to trust.
