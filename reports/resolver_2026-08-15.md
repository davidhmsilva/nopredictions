## 2. Team-mapping bugs — 4 trades priced against the wrong team ⚠️

This is the one that needs your attention. The scanners resolved PM market
titles to the wrong clubs, so the "edge" on these trades is meaningless.

| Trade | PM market says | Model priced | Reality |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2678 | Central Espanol FC (Uruguay) | **"Central African Republic"** — a national team | Central Español lost 0-1 to Cerro Largo |
| 4908 | Tigres FC (Colombia Primera B) | **Tigres UANL** (Liga MX) | different club, different country |
| 5128 | CA River Plate (Uruguay Segunda) | **River Plate** (Argentina) vs Indep. Rivadavia | different club, different country |
| 2625 | "Central Espanol vs Liverpool Montevideo" | — | **this fixture never existed**; Central Español played Cerro Largo, Liverpool played Defensor Sporting |

Trade 4908 reported a **+33.1pp edge**. An edge that large on a 1X2 market is
almost always a mapping error rather than a real mispricing — a cheap sanity
gate (reject edges > ~20pp on 1X2 pending manual review) would have caught it.

Trade 2625's market appears to be a **phantom PM fixture** (slug
`uru1-esp-liv-2026-07-25-liv`). It has sat at 0.495/0.505 since 07-25 and will
likely never resolve.

## Recommended actions, in priority order

1. **Fix the team mapping** in `fixture_match.py` / `team_aliases.json`. Three
   confirmed wrong-club matches in one 34-trade sample is a high rate, and every
   one of them produced a trade.
2. **Add an edge sanity gate** — reject 1X2 edges above ~20pp pending review.
   Would have blocked 4908 (+33.1pp) and 4889 (+20.6pp).
3. **Decide the FLB corner question.** Either confirm PM eventually settles
   these, or stop trading them — right now the strategy generates positions that
   cannot be scored.
4. **Refresh `resolution_time` from Gamma `endDate`** each resolver pass, so
   postponed fixtures stop re-appearing as due.
5. Optional: settle the 7 corner trades from real-world data. SQL below — but
   `clv` and `pm_clv` will stay null, and this records a result PM itself has
   not recognised.

```sql
-- Settle 7 FLB corner trades from api-football data. payout_units is GROSS.
UPDATE paper_trades SET result='won',  payout_units=stake_units*entry_odds, resolved_at=now() WHERE id IN (2594, 3488, 3529);
UPDATE paper_trades SET result='lost', payout_units=0,                      resolved_at=now() WHERE id IN (2438, 2665, 3806, 3850);
```
