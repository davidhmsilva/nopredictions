## Gaps (where nobody does "is this sports price wrong, explained in plain English, no tips")
1. **Everyone outputs a signal, not a reason.** "Edge +21c", "Copy Bet", "smart money" are all actions. Nobody writes why a price might be off (form, line-ups, similar matches, order book depth) in sentences.
2. **The price is only compared with other prices.** The odds tools compare against books or a model. None put a Polymarket price next to its own history, similar-match pricing and form vs closing line in one view.
3. **Whale tools sell following people.** That is the opposite of research. It is the PnL/receipt culture we already ruled out.
4. **The tone is hype.** "Guaranteed profit", "Bigger Profits", "before everyone else". Only ZenHodl ("not a promise of profit", 18+) and The Odds Gap (publish their own error) are measured. That tone is the empty spot.
5. **The tools are built for sharps.** Unabated/Bet Hero assume you know de-vig, CLV and EV. Nothing explains a Polymarket sports market to a smart non-quant.
6. **Coverage is patchy.** ZenHodl has no soccer, SportsBookISH is Kalshi-only, and most are US-league first. All sports, Polymarket-first, is open.

## Positioning draft (English)
**One-line positioning:**
No Predictions is the research layer for Polymarket sports: it checks whether a price looks wrong against history, order book, similar matches and line-ups, and explains it in plain English. No picks. No predictions. Just edges.

**3 differentiators:**
1. **Explains, doesn't signal.** Competitors give you a number to act on (edge %, whale to copy). We give a short brief on why the price and the evidence disagree, and you decide.
2. **The price vs its own context.** Not only "vs sportsbooks". We show the market's history, order book, how similar matches were priced, and form vs closing line, all side by side.
3. **Measured by design, every sport.** NFL, NBA, MLB, soccer and more, with no copy-trading, no "guaranteed" and no PnL screenshots. 18+.

**What NOT to claim:**
- No "guaranteed profit", "lock", "beat the market", "smart money" or "+EV" promises. No win rates or ROI.
- No "most accurate" or "only tool" claims we can't prove. Don't say competitors are wrong.
- No sport coverage we don't actually ship yet.
- No picks, even framed as "our lean". Say "the price looks high relative to X", never "bet Y".
- Always 18+ and "research, not advice".
