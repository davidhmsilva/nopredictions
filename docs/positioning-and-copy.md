# Positioning and copy

## Brand rules

- **All sports** — never present the product as single-sport.
- **No tips, no guaranteed profit.**
- **18+.**
- **Measured claims** only.

Proposed positioning line (awaiting David's OK — see [decisions.md](decisions.md)):
> The pre-trade check for sports prediction markets: both prices after fees, the context behind them, your call.

## Proposed site copy

### Hero
- **Hero:** "Every game on Kalshi and Polymarket. One board."
- **Sub:** "See which app lists your game, compare both prices with fees included, and open it in one tap. No tips. No trades. Just the numbers."
- **CTA:** "See today's games →"
- **Alt hero:** "Every game on Kalshi and Polymarket. Check it before you trade."

### How it works
1. **"Every game, both apps."** NFL, college football, NBA, MLB, NHL, WNBA and soccer, from Kalshi and Polymarket, on one schedule.
2. **"See where it's listed."** Each game shows which app has it, so you stop searching twice.
3. **"Check before you open the app."** Open a game to see both prices side by side, fees included. Then you place it yourself, on the app you choose.

### Card microcopy
- "On Kalshi + Polymarket" / "Only on Kalshi" / "Only on Polymarket"
- CTA: "Open game →"

### Pricing
- **Headline:** "The board is free. Pro is for research."
- **Sub:** "Every game, every sport, both apps: free, no card. Pro removes the daily limits on the research tools."

### Footer
"18+ · Research tool only: no tips, no trades, we never hold your money. Not betting or financial advice. Gambling problem? Call 1-800-GAMBLER."
